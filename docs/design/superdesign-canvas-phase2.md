## Concept

- Our phase-1 shell unchanged: sidebar (filter, local library, track rows)
  / visualization area / bottom transport; waveform progress strip with
  times on both ends.
- Visualization area grows mode tabs top-right: **LYRICS / VINYL /
  VISUALIZER** - the phase-2 visualizer mode switcher.
- VINYL mode centerpiece: a turntable deck card - plinth with corner
  screws, black vinyl with grooves, colored center label ("STEREO 33
  1/3 RPM"), tonearm with counterweight and cartridge, start/stop button
  below-left, vertical pitch slider on the deck's right edge.
- Transport gains a NOW PLAYING panel (label + "Track - Artist" + mini
  bars) and a rotary volume knob between volume icon and EQ/SCROB buttons
  (EQ and SCROB are phase 3).
